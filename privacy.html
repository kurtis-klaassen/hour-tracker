Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

TradesClock ("we", "our", "the app") is a time tracking application designed for trades workers and their employers. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, full name, and account type (worker or boss). When you use the app, we store your time entries including clock in/out times, break durations, job names, categories, and notes you add.

If your company uses them, we also store the parts and tools or machines logged on each job, and the photos and files you attach to your time entries. Bosses can also store pay and billing rates, prices, tax settings, business details, customer details for jobs, and invoices. All data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database service.

TradesClock does not collect your location.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the time tracking and invoicing service. Time entries, parts, tools, photos and files are stored so you can review your work history and share it with your employer if you choose to link your account. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Boss-Worker Data Sharing

If you are a worker and accept an invitation from a boss, that boss will be able to view your time entries, including hours, job names, notes, parts and tools used, and attached photos and files. Prices and rates set by your boss are only visible to your boss. You can disconnect from a boss at any time through the app settings. Bosses cannot modify your entries.

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase. Data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption. Access to your data is protected by your account credentials. We implement row-level security policies to ensure users can only access data they are authorized to view.

Your Rights

You can view all data associated with your account within the app. You can edit or delete individual time entries at any time. You can delete your entire account and all associated data permanently through the app settings. Account deletion removes all your entries, jobs, categories, and profile information.

Photos and Files

Photos and files you attach to time entries are stored privately with your account data. You can remove them at any time by editing the entry. They are only visible to you and, if connected, your boss.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, TradesClock reminds you on your phone if you have been clocked in for 10 hours. This reminder is created on your phone and no notification data is sent to us.

Children's Privacy

TradesClock is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
